# 03 - News & Catalyst Weighting Rules

## Scoring Rubric (1 to 10 Scale)
- **1-3 (Noise):** Routine commentary, recycled headlines, unconfirmed rumors -> *Ignore*
- **4-6 (Watchlist):** Material development with unclear timing or priced-in consensus -> *Monitor only*
- **7-8 (Actionable):** Verified fundamental, regulatory, or supply-chain shift with 1-6 week repricing window -> *Eligible for standard position*
- **9-10 (High Conviction):** Major structural shock, policy reversal, or bottleneck inflection -> *Eligible for max position sizing*

## Theme 1: AI Silicon & Supply Chain (Weight: High)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - NVIDIA regulatory, export-control, accounting, or supply-chain scrutiny
  - Hyperscaler capex revisions and custom ASIC shifts (e.g., Broadcom, Marvell)
  - Foundry/packaging bottlenecks (TSMC CoWoS) and HBM memory pricing
- **Mapping Logic:** Evaluate both direct impact on `NVDA` and second-order rotation into custom silicon, networking, or equipment suppliers.

## Theme 2: Data Center Power, Grid & Political Friction (Weight: High)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - Municipal or state ballot pushback, zoning moratoriums, and water/power restrictions on new data centers
  - Utility interconnection delays and FERC regulatory rulings
  - Behind-the-meter power agreements (nuclear IPPs, natural gas turbines, grid transformers, liquid cooling)
- **Mapping Logic:** Penalize exposed data-center REITs or delayed regional builds; overweight grid-equipment, power-generation, and thermal-management beneficiaries.

## Theme 3: Macro & Rate Sensitivity (Weight: Medium)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - 10-year US Treasury yield spikes (> 15 bps in a week)
  - Federal Reserve rate-path surprises or CPI/PCE prints outside consensus
- **Mapping Logic:** Tighten position sizing on high-multiple growth equities when real yields spike; favor cash-flow-positive infrastructure and defensive utilities.
