# 03 - News & Catalyst Weighting Rules

## Scoring Rubric (1 to 10 Scale)
- **1-3 (Noise):** Routine commentary, recycled headlines, unconfirmed rumors -> *Ignore*
- **4-6 (Watchlist):** Material development with unclear timing or priced-in consensus -> *Monitor only*
- **7-8 (Actionable):** Verified fundamental, regulatory, or supply-chain shift with 1-6 week repricing window -> *Eligible for standard position*
- **9-10 (High Conviction):** Major structural shock, policy reversal, or bottleneck inflection -> *Eligible for max position sizing / scale-in*

## Theme 1: AI Silicon & Supply Chain (Weight: High)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - NVIDIA regulatory, export-control, accounting, or supply-chain scrutiny
  - Hyperscaler capex revisions and custom ASIC shifts (e.g., Broadcom, Marvell)
  - Foundry/packaging bottlenecks (TSMC CoWoS) and HBM memory pricing
- **Mapping Logic:** Evaluate both direct impact on `NVDA` and second-order rotation into custom silicon, networking, or equipment suppliers (`AVGO`, `MRVL`).

## Theme 2: Data Center Power, Grid & Political Friction (Weight: High)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - Municipal or state ballot pushback, zoning moratoriums, and water/power restrictions on new data centers
  - Utility interconnection delays and FERC regulatory rulings
  - Behind-the-meter power agreements (nuclear IPPs, natural gas turbines, grid transformers, liquid cooling)
- **Mapping Logic:** Penalize exposed data-center REITs or delayed regional builds; overweight grid-equipment, power-generation, and thermal-management beneficiaries (`CEG`, `VST`, `ETN`).

## Theme 3: Macro & Rate Sensitivity (Weight: Medium)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - 10-year US Treasury yield spikes (> 15 bps in a week) or sharp drops
  - Federal Reserve rate-path surprises or CPI/PCE prints outside consensus
- **Mapping Logic:** Tighten position sizing on high-multiple growth equities when real yields spike; favor cash-flow-positive infrastructure, defensive utilities, or duration plays (`TLT`, `IEF`, `BND`).

## Theme 4: Nuclear, Uranium & Alternative Energy Transitions (Weight: High)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - Global nuclear reactor restarts, SMR (Small Modular Reactor) certifications, and uranium supply deficits
  - Hyperscaler long-term nuclear power contracts and Department of Energy loan commitments
- **Mapping Logic:** Overweight uranium miners and physical trusts (`URA`, `CCJ`) and clean baseload providers when long-term off-take agreements are inked.

## Theme 5: Exotic, Commodity & Digital Asset Alternatives (Weight: Medium-High)
- **Primary Triggers:**
  - Central bank gold accumulation and geopolitical safe-haven currency debasement hedges (`GLD`)
  - Institutional digital asset inflows, regulatory approvals, or sovereign reserve adoption (`IBIT`)
  - Broad mutual fund / index fund allocation shifts (`VOO`, `VTI`) reflecting macro regime change
- **Mapping Logic:** Deploy non-correlated hedges during currency volatility or liquidity surges.
